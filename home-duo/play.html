How to Play

🎯
Goal Land one of your pieces into your target home ring (Green lands in Blue's ring; Blue lands in Green's ring).
↔️
Sliding Moves Pieces slide horizontally or vertically through open tiles. You cannot jump over or land on other pieces.
⛔
Home Protection Pieces can never land on or pass through their own starting home target ring.
🔄
Loop Breaking Rule You cannot make a move that recreates an earlier board position. Forbidden loop moves are marked in red and blocked.
⏱️
Turn Clock You have 60 seconds per turn to decide and execute your move.
